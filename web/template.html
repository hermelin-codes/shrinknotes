    How does it work?

    GoodNotes exports every pen stroke as hundreds of tiny overlapping shapes. ShrinkNotes merges them into one clean outline per stroke. The handwriting stays vector, so it is sharp at any zoom. Other compressors turn your pages into images instead.

    Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Privacy

    Your PDF never leaves your device. It is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

    The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To count visits it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn't build personal profiles. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the page.
